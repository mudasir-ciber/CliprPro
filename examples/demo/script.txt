# CliprPro Phase 2 Demo

What if a video engine could understand your script and your voice?

CliprPro starts with a simple idea: the AI does not paint pixels. It builds timed scenes from narration.

First the system listens to the voiceover and maps every sentence onto the audio timeline.

Then it detects pauses, finds the hook, and scores what matters most.

Finally it renders a deterministic Canvas scene and merges your original voice into a playable MP4.

Remember: Phase 3 will add AI storyboards. Today the timeline is the contract that makes that possible.
